One line of text (max 240 characters) on a free and entertaining theme, entered in make.fun's "One Line, Best Line" bounty. The entry is a single sentence about a password manager that locks its owner out of their own life and holds their memories hostage for a ransom of one uppercase letter and a special character — it opens on a relatable image, stays one sentence, and lands the joke on the punch at the end.

> My password manager just locked me out of my own life and is now holding my memories hostage for a ransom of one uppercase letter and a special character.

`main.ts` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`, where the gate scorer checks it (UTF-8, 1–240 characters) and the AI judge compares it pairwise against the other entries.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `./output/entry.txt` when `/output` is not writable, e.g. on a local machine). Requires Node 22 or later; no dependencies to install.
